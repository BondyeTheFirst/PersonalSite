Three stories

The numbers above came from real problems. Here is how three of them went.

From conflicting figures to one source of truth

The problem. Data lived in siloed systems, pipelines created duplicated datasets, and stakeholders were the ones finding bugs, daily.

What I did. Surveyed users, then led a data quality framework, better documentation, a unified data platform and automated alerts that opened tickets before anyone had to.

The result. Bug tickets down about 35% and data tool usage up 20%.

Data for people who can’t write SQL

The problem. Senior stakeholders waited days for simple answers, and the reporting team was buried in requests.

What I did. Ran a proof of concept of Databricks Genie for leadership, including C-levels, and championed it across the business.

The result. Time to a simple insight fell from days to minutes, requests nearly halved, and Genie dashboards became official reporting tools.

Claims data you can trace

The problem. In insurance, every euro moves from first notification to settlement, and Finance and Risk need to trust each figure.

What I do. Own Claims Data end to end, from reserves and payments to loss ratios and carrier reporting, and get five teams to agree on shared definitions.

The result. Numbers that reconcile and trace back to their source.
